Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a plain-language template; have it reviewed by a lawyer before relying on it commercially, and replace the contact line below.

What we collect

Your email address and a hashed password (we never store the password itself), and the data you enter: projects, clients, hourly rates, time entries, invoices and business details.

How we use it

To run the service: sign you in, store and show your data, generate invoices and reports, and let you share project links with clients. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI features

When you use AI quick-add or “Ask your data”, the text you type (or the relevant parts of your timesheet data) is sent to Anthropic's Claude to produce the result. It is sent only when you click the button.

Who can see your data

  • Only you, unless you choose to share.
  • Anyone with a client-portal link you create can see that project's hours, sent invoices and (if a rate is set) amounts, until you revoke the link.
  • If you join a team, the team owner can see your tracked hours, billable time and earnings totals (not your entry descriptions).

Where it is stored

In a Turso (SQLite) database hosted through Vercel, and in your browser's local storage as an offline cache. Sessions use a secure, HttpOnly cookie.

Your choices

You can download all your data (Account → Download all my data) and permanently delete your account and all associated data (Account → Delete my account) at any time.
